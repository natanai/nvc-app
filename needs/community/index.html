Need for Community

What a powerful experience it can be for us when our need for community is met. To experience inclusion, belonging, and often a sense of a purpose greater than any single person. There can be a lot of pain in even admitting to ourselves that we have this need for community that wants to be included in our strategies. It can be overwhelming to us. As if it is an insurmountable task to tend to this need.

Strategies

Try Meetup

Meetup is a website that tries to connect you to people with similar interests. Worth a shot?

Play a video game

There is such a wide diversity of video games out there. Calming exploratory games, puzzle games, combat, story, single or multiplayer, etc. Once I get in touch with the need that is alive in me it’s fun to look for a game or game genre that might tend specifically to that.
