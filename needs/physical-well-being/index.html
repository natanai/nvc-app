Need for Physical Well-Being

Strategies

Sip water slowly

Pause and take ten tiny sips, noticing the temperature and sensation.

Neck release

Roll your head in small circles; then tilt ear to shoulder for three breaths each side.

Shoulder rolls

Roll shoulders forward ten times, then back ten times, noticing any change.

Shake for 30 seconds

Set a timer and gently shake arms, hands, and legs to discharge tension.

Wall push-ups

Do five slow wall push-ups; feel your strength and breathing.

Back to wall lean

Press your spine to a wall and breathe into your back for 5 breaths.

4-7-8 breathing

Inhale 4, hold 7, exhale 8 (four cycles).

Legs up the wall

Lay with calves on a chair or wall for two minutes.

Window minute

Open a window and take five fuller breaths of outside air.

Barefoot on grass

If available, stand barefoot on grass or carpet for 30 seconds.

Two-minute eye rest

Close eyes or look at a far point for two minutes.

Jaw unclench

Place tongue on the floor of your mouth; relax jaw for 10 breaths.

Gentle sway

Stand or sit and sway side-to-side with your breath.

Light adjust

Increase or dim the light to suit your body right now.

Fresh socks

Change into clean or cozy socks for a reset.

Posture reset

Sit or stand tall; lengthen spine; soften shoulders.

One-foot balance

Stand on one foot near support for 20–30 seconds.

One gentle stretch chain

Reach up, fold forward, roll up—three times, slowly.

Choose a supportive spot

Move to a seat with back support or softer light.
