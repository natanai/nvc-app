Need for Play

Strategies

Color hunt

Pick one color and find five items of that color around you.

One-minute doodle

Draw lines, dots, or shapes continuously for 60 seconds.

Haiku in a minute

Write a 5-7-5 poem about what you notice.

Three shapes doodle

Draw one circle, one triangle, one square repeatedly for a minute.

One-foot balance

Stand on one foot near support for 20–30 seconds.

Future-walk sketch

Pick a nearby block on a map and sketch a simple future walking loop.

Micro-celebration

Clap once or do a tiny fist-pump after finishing a task.
