Alexithymia Support

About one in ten people live with alexithymia. If naming a feeling is hard, this step-by-step lane offers gentle structure so you can notice your body, explore possible emotions, and choose caring next steps.

Optional pause: settle your body

If you feel revved up, try a short breathing cycle before checking in. Slow, counted breaths can help your nervous system shift toward safety.

Step 4: Reflect and journal

Linking a feeling to context strengthens emotional awareness. Use the prompts below as writing cues or simply sit with them.

  • What was happening right before you noticed this feeling?
  • Does the emotion you selected fit? What signals line up or feel different?
  • How intense is it right now on a scale from 1 (barely there) to 10 (all-consuming)?
  • What do you need or long for in this moment?
